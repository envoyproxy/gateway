Fixed a route-level `SecurityPolicy`, `BackendTrafficPolicy` or `EnvoyExtensionPolicy` with `mergeType` set reporting its status on two ancestors for the same parent when the route's parentRef has no `sectionName`. The merge results (`Merged`, or `Accepted: False` when the merged policy failed to translate) went on one ancestor per listener, while `Accepted: True` went on the Gateway or ListenerSet itself, so a policy whose merge failed reported both `Accepted: False` and `Accepted: True`. The merge results of all listeners are now reported on the parentRef's ancestor, the same one used without `mergeType`.
