The listener-level `ext_authz` HTTP filters are no longer named after the `SecurityPolicy` (`envoy.filters.http.ext_authz/<policy>`). They are now named by a hash of their filter-level settings, and their authorization service moved to the route's `typed_per_filter_config`. Remote JWKS clusters for JWT providers that use a `backendRef` are likewise renamed from `<policy>/jwt/<index>` to `jwks/<hash>`, which changes their cluster stat names, and they no longer carry the policy's resource metadata because policies that share a JWKS backend now share the cluster. An `EnvoyPatchPolicy` or dashboard that targets these filters or clusters by their old names must be updated.
