Added a `serviceSettings` field under `EnvoyGateway.spec.rateLimit` to configure connection-level behavior for the global rate limit service cluster, such as `circuitBreaker.maxRequestsPerConnection` and `timeout.http.maxConnectionDuration`. It exposes only the settings that apply to the managed rate limit service (TCP keepalive, GRPC/TCP active and passive health checks, circuit breakers, connection timeouts and HTTP/2 settings), and they are validated at config-load time, so an invalid value is rejected at startup instead of only once a Global rate limit policy is used.
