Fixed `BackendTrafficPolicy`/`ClientTrafficPolicy` `ClusterSettings` merge-eligibility detection for HTTP, GRPC, TCP, and UDP routes to consider each Gateway/ListenerSet listener the route actually attaches to, instead of relying on the route's literal `sectionName`. Previously, a listener-scoped `ClusterSettings` setting could be missed (or a clean listener could be over-cautiously treated as diverging) whenever a route's `parentRef` omitted `sectionName`, producing incorrect `mergeBackends` cluster sharing/splitting decisions.
