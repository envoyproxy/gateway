Adding, editing or removing a Lua `EnvoyExtensionPolicy` no longer drains the listener. The scripts travel with the routes, so a policy change is a route configuration update rather than a listener rebuild. The listener carries placeholder Lua filter slots allocated in blocks of 10, sized to the longest Lua chain on any of its routes. It is still rebuilt when a Gateway gets its first Lua policy or loses its last one, or when that longest chain crosses a multiple of 10, for example growing from 10 scripts to 11.
