`ExtensionServerPolicy` can now target a `GatewayClass` (via `targetRefs` or `targetSelectors`) when `mergeGateways` is enabled, attaching the policy to every listener of the merged Gateways, or to the listeners with a matching name when `sectionName` is set. Targeting a `GatewayClass` without `mergeGateways`, or with a `sectionName` that matches no listener, now reports an `Accepted=False` status instead of being silently ignored.
