Added `BackendTrafficPolicy.spec.http1` (`BackendHTTP1Settings`) for configuring upstream (backend) HTTP/1 settings such as header-case preservation and trailer support. When both `BackendTrafficPolicy.spec.http1` and the deprecated `ClientTrafficPolicy.spec.http1` flat fields are set, `BackendTrafficPolicy` takes precedence for backend (upstream) traffic. HTTP/1 settings for non-xRoute backends (ext-auth, ext-proc, OIDC, JWT) are not yet supported via `spec.http1`.

Added `ClientTrafficPolicy.spec.http1.client` (`ClientHTTP1Settings`) as the new canonical way to configure downstream (listener) HTTP/1 settings. The flat fields on `spec.http1` (`enableTrailers`, `preserveHeaderCase`, `http10`, `disableSafeMaxConnectionDuration`, `ignoredUpgradeTypes`) are now deprecated in favor of their equivalents under `spec.http1.client`.
