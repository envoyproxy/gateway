The jwt_authn requirement generated from a JWT `SecurityPolicy` is now named after the SecurityPolicy that owns the providers, `<namespace>/<name>[:missing|:missing-or-failed]`, instead of a hash of its content. Routes that share a policy still share one requirement. An `EnvoyPatchPolicy` that targets a requirement by its hash-based name needs to use the new name. The names change once on upgrade, so requests on connections opened before it may be refused with 403 `Wrong requirement_name` until those connections drain.
