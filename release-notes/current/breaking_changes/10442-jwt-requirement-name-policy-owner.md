The jwt_authn requirement generated from a JWT `SecurityPolicy` is now named after the SecurityPolicy that applies to the route, `<namespace>/<name>`, or for a merged policy after the route and parent policies it was built from, `<route namespace>/<route name>+<parent namespace>/<parent name>`, instead of the provider names and a hash of its content (`<provider>[-or-<provider>...][-missing|-missing-or-failed]_<hash>`). Routes that share a policy still share one requirement. An `EnvoyPatchPolicy` that targets a requirement by its hash-based name needs to use the new name. The names change once on upgrade, so requests on connections opened before it may be refused with 403 `Wrong requirement_name` until those connections drain.
